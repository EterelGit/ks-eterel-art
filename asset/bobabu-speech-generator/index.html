Asset Overview

Bring your game's characters to life with delightful, expressive babble speech! Inspired by beloved classics like **Animal Crossing**, **Undertale**, **Celeste**, and **Banjo-Kazooie**, **Bo-Ba-Bu Speech Generator** procedural dialogue vocalization system that gives every character a unique voice—without the cost of voice actors or cloud AI subscriptions. Synchronized in real-time with character dialogue text, Bo-Ba-Bu dynamically modulates pitch, tempo, and vocal timbre based on punctuation, questions, exclamations, ALL-CAPS shouting, and inline emotion tags (`[Joy]`, `[Angry]`, `[Whisper]`, `[Loving]`, `[Cunning]`, `[Confused]`, and more). Whether you are making an RPG, a cozy life simulator, a visual novel, or a story-driven adventure, Bo-Ba-Bu provides an instant, charming vocal presence for all your NPCs and protagonists. ### ✨ Key Features * **67 Ready-to-Use Voice Profiles:** Includes 10 organic 3-letter natural voices (`Voice3_`), cute sprites, grumpy gnomes, robotic droids, wizards, warriors, and monsters. * **Inline Emotion Modulation:** Add tags like `[Joy]`, `[Fury]`, `[Whisper]`, `[Loving]`, `[Cunning]`, or `[EvilLaugh]` directly into your text to trigger emotional vocal shifts on the fly. * **Punctuation & Syntax Awareness:** Automatically handles question inflection (`?`), exclamation emphasis (`!`), comma pauses (`,`), sentence stops, ellipses (`...`), and volume/tempo boosts for ALL-CAPS text. * **Universal Text Component Support:** Works seamlessly with both **Legacy UI Text** and **TextMeshPro (`TMP_Text`)**. * **Bo-Ba-BU Studio Editor:** A dedicated editor window to audition single syllables, test sentences, customize pitch/tempo, and create your own custom emotion tags directly in Edit Mode without entering Play Mode. * **SpeechTranslatorBoBaBu:** Automatically cleans emotion tags from the displayed dialogue box while sending emotional cues to the speaker. * **Two Interactive Demo Scenes Included:** 1. *Classic Dialogue Stage:* 3-speaker turn-based conversation with random scenarios and syllable rate switcher. 2. *Two-Sided Persona Stage:* Visual novel two-sided stage featuring 4 illustrated characters dynamically swapping when speaking. * **100% Offline & Lightweight:** Pure algorithmic digital signal processing and pre-rendered WAV banks. Zero external cloud APIs, zero subscription fees, and minimal CPU overhead.
