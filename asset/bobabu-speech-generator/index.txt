Synchronized in real-time with character dialogue text, Bo-Ba-Bu dynamically modulates pitch, tempo, and vocal timbre based on punctuation, questions, exclamations, ALL-CAPS shouting, and inline emotion tags (`[Joy]`, `[Angry]`, `[Whisper]`, `[Loving]`, `[Cunning]`, `[Confused]`, and more).

Whether you are making an RPG, a cozy life simulator, a visual novel, or a story-driven adventure, Bo-Ba-Bu provides an instant, charming vocal presence for all your NPCs and protagonists.

### ✨ Key Features

* **67 Ready-to-Use Voice Profiles:** Includes 10 organic 3-letter natural voices (`Voice3_`), cute sprites, grumpy gnomes, robotic droids, wizards, warriors, and monsters.
* **Inline Emotion Modulation:** Add tags like `[Joy]`, `[Fury]`, `[Whisper]`, `[Loving]`, `[Cunning]`, or `[EvilLaugh]` directly into your text to trigger emotional vocal shifts on the fly.
* **Punctuation & Syntax Awareness:** Automatically handles question inflection (`?`), exclamation emphasis (`!`), comma pauses (`,`), sentence stops, ellipses (`...`), and volume/tempo boosts for ALL-CAPS text.
* **Universal Text Component Support:** Works seamlessly with both **Legacy UI Text** and **TextMeshPro (`TMP_Text`)**.
* **Bo-Ba-BU Studio Editor:** A dedicated editor window to audition single syllables, test sentences, customize pitch/tempo, and create your own custom emotion tags directly in Edit Mode without entering Play Mode.
* **SpeechTranslatorBoBaBu:** Automatically cleans emotion tags from the displayed dialogue box while sending emotional cues to the speaker.
* **Two Interactive Demo Scenes Included:**

  2. *Two-Sided Persona Stage:* Visual novel two-sided stage featuring 4 illustrated characters dynamically swapping when speaking.
