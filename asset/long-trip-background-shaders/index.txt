13:Tabe,**Long Trip — turn your background into a journey.**

Create flowing neon tunnels, cosmic landscapes, connected light networks and hypnotic geometric patterns with LongTrip Background Shaders for Unity URP.

Explore 83 ready-to-use material presets across 6 specialized shader engines. Use them as a starting point, customize their appearance and motion, and build animated backgrounds for menus, sci-fi environments, portals, arcade games and stylized visual experiences.

**Procedural visuals. No texture assets required.**

LongTrip generates its patterns directly in shaders, from geometric frames and glowing grids to nebula clouds, crystal structures and retro pixel effects. Adjust the materials to create variations without preparing textures or video loops.

**Six engines, a wide range of styles**

• **Master:** geometric tunnels, perspective corridors, honeycomb patterns, hyperspace streaks and raymarched structures.
• **Tunnels Plus:** cloud vortices, architectural passages, laser corridors, jewel portals and flowing ribbon tunnels.
• **Plexus & Lines:** connected constellations, wireframe terrain, optical fibers, circuit trails and neon beams.
• **Grid & Blocks:** animated tiles, building blocks, mazes, mosaics and kinetic geometric patterns.
• **Cosmic & Organic:** nebulae, crystal shards, sphere swarms, neural networks and black-hole visuals.
• **Pixel Art:** crisp retro tunnels, arcade corridors, pixel portals, kaleidoscopes and clocks.

**Shape the look and motion**

Customize HDR colors, palette cycling, glow, geometry density, line thickness and depth layers. Control animation speed, rotation, scrolling, zoom and pattern position, with additional settings for individual effects.

Use custom time input to synchronize materials or drive their animation from your own scripts.

**Layer particles and transitions**

Add procedural starfields, radial sparks or particle waves through the optional overlay module. Create iris, spiral and hex-grid transitions with adjustable progress and glowing edges.

Optional finishing effects include chromatic aberration, vignette and CRT scanlines.

**Preview, explore and customize**

Browse presets using the included Remote Control Panel and demo showcase. Switch categories, adjust materials, save custom defaults and hide the showcase HUD for a clean view.

Apply materials to quads, planes or UV-mapped meshes to bring the effects into your scenes.

**Built for Unity URP**

Includes 83 material presets, 6 shader engines, an editor control panel, a demo showcase and a user manual.
