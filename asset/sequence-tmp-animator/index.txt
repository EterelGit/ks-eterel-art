13:T87d,**Sequence TMP Animator** brings expressive motion to TextMesh Pro UI and 3D text. Animate individual characters or entire strings, combine effects, and build multi-step sequences directly in the Unity Inspector.

Create animated titles, dialogue reveals, notifications, menu labels and world-space text with curve-controlled motion and adjustable character timing.

**Eight configurable effects**

- **Fade** — animate character transparency.
- **Scale** — control horizontal and vertical scale around each glyph’s center.
- **Jump** — add curve-controlled vertical movement.
- **Shake** — apply animated displacement.
- **Offset** — control horizontal and vertical movement independently.
- **Rotation** — rotate characters around their centers.
- **Text Curve** — bend text along a custom curve.
- **Scramble** — cycle through replacement characters while preserving rich-text tags.

**Compose multi-step animations**

Arrange and reorder steps, then play them sequentially or simultaneously. Configure delays, durations and animation curves, stagger characters from left to right or right to left, and loop individual animations or complete sequences.

**Preview directly in the Editor**

Test a single animation or a complete sequence in Edit Mode. Preview restores the original text geometry when the Inspector closes, helping you experiment safely while working on your scene.

**Control playback from your game**

Use Play On Awake for automatic playback or trigger animations through the runtime Play and Stop API. Configure finite or unlimited loops, hold the final pose during loop delays, and smoothly restore the original text after playback.

**UI and 3D text support**

Use the same animation system with TextMeshProUGUI and world-space TextMeshPro. Effects operate directly on TMP mesh data and work with Built-in, URP and HDRP projects.

**Showcase and documentation included**
