13:T4fe,Smoke Flow Effects KS gives you two ways to add 2D smoke to your Unity project. Use Physical FX when smoke needs to flow around level geometry or react to moving objects. Use 2D Cartoon for stylized plumes, glow, and soft mist driven by an animated material.

**SmokeFlow Physical FX**

Simulate density and velocity on a local CPU grid. Closed 2D colliders can block the flow, and moving influencers can push smoke, leave wakes, or emit density. Adjust wind, buoyancy, turbulence, and dissipation in the Inspector. Map density to a color gradient and switch between smooth and pixel-art filtering. Multiple nearby emitters can share the same domain, with Mobile, Balanced, and High presets to tune simulation cost.

**SmokeFlow 2D Cartoon**

Start with Alpha Blend, Additive Glow, Soft Mist, or Shaped Flow. Adjust tint, opacity, density, plume width, rise, drift, sway, and warp through the material. Use the included uGUI Image prefab or assign a material to a quad. Procedural animation runs in the shader without a CPU flow grid.

**Two interactive Smoke Lab demos**

Drag obstacles, adjust wind, and compare three independent Physical domains. In the Cartoon demo, tune and pause each preset independently and switch between dark and light backgrounds to compare blending.14:T740,- **Unity version** — Unity 6 (6000.0+). - **Physical rendering** — Density gradient with adjustable opacity, plus Bilinear or Point filtering. The included demo uses URP 2D Unlit. Custom materials must sample the sprite texture.
- **Cartoon rendering** — Unlit procedural URP shaders with Alpha Blend, Additive, and Premultiplied variants. Four material presets for quads and uGUI Image. The Cartoon shaders do not support uGUI stencil masks or RectMask2D clipping.
- **Included content** — Two interactive demo scenes, runtime scripts, shaders and materials, sample prefabs including a Cartoon particle example, and documentation.
